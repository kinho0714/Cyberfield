The five PNGs in `sprites/` were copied byte-for-byte from the user-supplied archive.
The names `scrap_blade`, `breaker_maul`, `arc_saber`, `arc_emitter`, and
`pulse_carbine` are Cyberfield gameplay names, not original sprite titles.
Credit Reactorcore when publishing any build with these sprites.

## Arte original (01/11/2026)

`sprites_v2/` (bastão, cano, pistolas, escopeta, rifle de pulso, arco neon) e
`projectiles/` (projéteis e impactos) são pixel art **original** gerada por
`tools/visual/arsenal_art.py` (determinístico, sem pixels de terceiros). O mesmo script gera
`assets/fx/arsenal/`, `assets/cyberware/` e `assets/accessories/`.
